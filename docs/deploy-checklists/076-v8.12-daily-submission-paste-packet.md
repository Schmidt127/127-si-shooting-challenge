## FUT-041 — XP Earned | Extra Credit (template columns)

Hub daily email renders two metric columns from 076 payload:

| Column | Payload field | Source in 076 | Notes |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **XP Earned** | `xpEarned` | Sum of active XP Events on Submission where Source Key starts with `SUBMISSION_XP\|` | `null` + `submissionXpStatus: "Pending / not yet awarded"` when not yet awarded |
| **Extra Credit** | `xpExtraCredit` | Always **`0`** from 076 | No daily extra-credit store yet — column always shows **0** |

Backward compatibility: **`submissionXp`** mirrors **`xpEarned`**. Full mapping: [`FUT-041-daily-submission-xp-columns.md`](./FUT-041-daily-submission-xp-columns.md).

**Out of scope:** automation **010** award logic, homework extra credit on Game Log, Hub homework/video templates.
